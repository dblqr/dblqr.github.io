How It Works
  1. Enter up to 6 friend codes by typing them in, scanning QR codes, or uploading a text file.
  2. The app generates a time-stamped QR code for each friend code that the Shenron and Porunga events can scan in-game.
  3. Each code counts once per day and the event needs 3 a day. Tap Start daily scans to step through your codes one at a time; extra codes are spares if one doesn't work. Progress resets with the game at 1:00 AM Pacific time.
  4. QR codes auto-refresh every 10 seconds to stay valid for the game. Tap a QR code to enlarge it for scanning.
  5. Share your codes with friends by exporting to a .txt file or letting them scan your screen.
  6. Downloaded QR images contain the time they were made, so they may stop working after a while. Download a fresh one when you need it.
